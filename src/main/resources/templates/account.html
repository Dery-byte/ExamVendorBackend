Who can sign in

Everyone below signs in with a code emailed to them; there are no passwords. Add or remove people directly in the database, as with the exam portal's developer_email table:

INSERT INTO vendor_user (email, name, created_at) VALUES ('name@example.com', 'Their Name', NOW());
DELETE FROM vendor_user WHERE email = 'name@example.com';
